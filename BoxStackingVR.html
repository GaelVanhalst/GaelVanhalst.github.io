Palletizing App

Pick the boxes, place the boxes, stack the boxes and if nobody is watching, maybe even throw the boxes! Welcome to your new job as box stacker! It is your mission to fill the carts with boxes, but be quick, follow the rules and be efficient with the space in the carts! You will get taught how to do your job well, and then you can go prove yourself. If you get good grades, you might even be employee of the month!
In this Virtual Reality experience you will use your hands to pick up boxes and place them into carts. Here you will first be taught how to do it, and then afterwards you have to prove yourself in some tests where you will get graded based on following the rules of the boxes, time and how efficient you have used your space.

This Virtual Reality project was made in DAE Research. I worked on it as a sole programmer together with one artist.
