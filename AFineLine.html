A Fine Line

In this small, but challenging adventure you will have to traverse a dangerous world filled with obstacles. Use your environment to get passed all the obstacles that block your path, so that you can finally get to that special person.

A Fine Line is a small puzzle platformer that I made for a 48h gamejam called the LoveJam in 2017.
You can play it here or download it from Itch.io.

I mainly used this project as an opportunity to improve my skills with the 2D part of Unity and also to work on my level and puzzle design skills.
