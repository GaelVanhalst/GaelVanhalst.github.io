S4S Eleonora's Pharmacy

Discover 18th centrury medicine recipes from Eleonora Verbeke. In this Augmented Reality application you can go through the physical pages of a book while seeing the corresponding scanned in pages from the "Winckelbouck" including extra visualizations of the ingredients. Next to this you will also have the chance to actually mix one of these historic recipes from the book.

This Augmented Reality project was made for the research project Scan4Stories at DAE Research. I worked on it as a sole programmer together with one artist.
