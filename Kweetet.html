Kweetet

I worked at Die Keure on Kweetet.be as a programmer. Originally I worked there as an intern for 4 months, but I later joined the team as an employee. During this period I worked on the general game, mini-games, tools and more.

Kweetet is a platform for schools with a game made in Unity. On this platform children can make their homework and do exercises to earn a virtual currency called Kwetons. This is a currency they need to play through the story of Kweetet.
The game itself is an adventure game were you have to do quests and play mini-games to uncover the mysteries about the world the player is in. The story is split into 6 years that are all split into 5 acts.
