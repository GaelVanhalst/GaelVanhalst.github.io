Molehill Shader

This is an HLSL shader I made for school in 2014.

The purpose of this shader is to make molehills in the gound. This geometry shader also creates extra polygons on the sides of the molehill, so that the molehill stays smooth on the sides and that it doesn't suddenly end with a weird angle. This makes that the shader can be used for both low poly and high poly planes.
