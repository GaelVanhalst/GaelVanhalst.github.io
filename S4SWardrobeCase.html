S4S Dress to impress

You have been invited to a high class party, but you can't just arrive in your normal clothes, can you? Go through a wardrobe and pick the clothes that you think will impress the host, or maybe even do the opposite.
In this Azure Kinect experience you will choose between different pieces of clothing to assemble your outfit. The avatar on screen follows your movements, so move around and see how the clothes look from every angle. Then as a finisher, you can pose with your outfit at different locations. Dress to impress!

This Azure Kinect project was made for the research project Scan4Stories at DAE Research. I worked on it as a sole programmer together with one artist.
