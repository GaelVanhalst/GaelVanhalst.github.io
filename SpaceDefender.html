Space Defender

Destroy them all and keep space safe for another day!

This is a game I made as a school assignment in 2013. It was made in a custom engine using c++.

In this game you steer a spaceship that you need to safely manouver through all obstacles, while destroying all the enemies. You destroy the enemy by using special rockets that you have to steer yourself, but watch out, because when you steer the rocket you don't have control over your ship, so hide or attack fast.
