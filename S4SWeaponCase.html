S4S Museum of the Future

Welcome to the Museum of the Future! Here you can see and hold some ancient weapons. If only we knew how these weapons were used. Can you help by demonstrating?
In this Virtual Reality experience you will use three different weapons, with a controller or a 3D printed version of one of the weapons. Each weapon has its own unique minigame, from cutting wood, to stabbing balloon knights. Can you handle them all?

This Virtual Reality project was made for the research project Scan4Stories at DAE Research. I worked on it as a sole programmer together with one artist.
