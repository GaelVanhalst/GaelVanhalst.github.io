Color Of Sin

A knight defending the shrine of his fallen female companion!

This game was part of a gamejam in 2014 and was made in a custom school engine in c++.

It was made by:
Laura Zapata Morales-Baumgarten (Artist)
Jan Van Hassel (Artist)
Andreas Van Vooren (Programmer)
Nicolas van Hal (Programmer)
Gaël Vanhalst (Programmer)

Music track : Clipped version of "Full On" by Kevin Macleod (incompetech.com)
Sound effects are edited versions from clips from various users on Freesound.org
