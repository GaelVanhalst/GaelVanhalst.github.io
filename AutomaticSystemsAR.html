Automatic Systems AR App

Go through the product catalog of Automatic Systems, discover the gates in AR, modify your gate-setup to what you need and then share your creation through picture and text!

I worked on this Augmented Reality Application at MOAR Studios, with Automatic Systems as our client. I was initially hired by MOAR Studios to help them finish this application, with my extensive knowledge of AR.

Some of the main things I have done for this app were:

  • Improving general stability of the application
  • Making the AR experience smoother and more stable
  • Make the app compatible with IOS
  • Ability to share multiple screenshots with a description of the content
  • Publish the app on the AppStore and Google Play
  • ...
