Bunny Annah Stones

Go jump into the cave of teasure! May you find riches beyond imagination!

This game was a groupswork that we made in 2015-2016 with unity. We entered the first ever Belgian Game Development Championship (BeGDC) with this game and currently we are in the finals.

It was made by:
Kwambo Kim (Artist)
Jan Van Hassel (Artist)
Jeroen Van der Veken (Programmer)
Gaël Vanhalst (Programmer)

I did (programming wise) the level generation, the powerups, the traps, the destructable grounds, the chance mechanisms, and more.

During the development we kept a blog were you can find more information on the evolution of the game and also what exactly I did for the game. Blog
